## 4. Full Gig Description

Stop losing potential customers to slow response times or overwhelming your support team with repetitive questions. 

Generic ChatGPT wrappers guess, hallucinate false answers, and invent policies you never wrote. **Your business needs a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant that is strictly grounded in your verified company data.**

I will build a custom **RAG Chatbot** trained directly on your business documents (PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets, markdown, handbooks) and your live website URLs. Every answer is backed by **verbatim source citations**, and if the answer isn't in your knowledge base, the bot politely declines and routes to human support.

### What Your Custom Bot Can Do:
- 📄 **Multi-Source Ingestion**: PDFs, customer service FAQs, employee handbooks, product catalogs, and website URLs.
- 🎯 **Verbatim Source Citations**: Shows clickable references (document name, page number, similarity score) beneath every answer.
- 🛡️ **Anti-Hallucination Guardrails**: Strict context boundaries ensure the AI never invents facts, prices, or false commitments.
- 🌐 **Floating Website Widget**: Embeds onto your WordPress, Shopify, Next.js, or custom site with a simple one-line script snippet.
- ⚡ **Hybrid Semantic Search**: Combines Dense Vector Embeddings (Cosine Similarity) with BM25 Keyword Matching for precision accuracy.
- 🔌 **API & Cloud Deployment**: Deployable as a Python/FastAPI backend, Docker container, or self-hosted cloud service (AWS, DigitalOcean, Vercel).

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How do you prevent the AI from hallucinating or making up answers?
**A:** We use strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The LLM is instructed to answer *only* using retrieved excerpts from your verified documents. If the requested information is absent, the bot triggers a guardrail that honestly states the answer is not in the knowledge base.

### Q: Can customers see where the answers come from?
**A:** Yes! Every response includes clickable source citations indicating the exact document name, page number, and relevant excerpt.

### Q: How easy is it to embed on my website?
**A:** Extremely simple. I provide a clean, lightweight JavaScript snippet that you can paste into your website header or footer (WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, React, HTML).

### Q: Can my sensitive company documents stay private?
**A:** Absolutely. I can build local offline vector processing or connect to private enterprise cloud endpoints with zero training retention.

### Q: Can I add or update documents in the future?
**A:** Yes. The ingestion manager allows you to drag-and-drop new files or crawl new URLs anytime to update the vector index.
